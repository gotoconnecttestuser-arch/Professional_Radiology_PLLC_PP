SMS Privacy Policy

Professional Radiology PLLC
Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Professional Radiology PLLC (“Professional Radiology,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information related to our text messaging communications.

By opting in to receive SMS messages from Professional Radiology PLLC, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from us, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name
- Appointment and scheduling information
- Message interaction data, including delivery confirmations and responses
- Any information you provide in text communications with us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use SMS messaging to provide customer care and healthcare-related communications, including:
- Appointment reminders
- Scheduling confirmations and updates
- Responses to patient inquiries
- Requests for additional scheduling or appointment information
- Important service-related notifications

SMS messages are not used for marketing or promotional purposes unless you separately consent.

3. SMS Consent

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from Professional Radiology PLLC. Message frequency may vary depending on your appointments and interactions with our office.

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

4. Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:
- STOP to cancel SMS messages
- HELP for assistance

After you send STOP, you may receive one final message confirming your opt-out status.

5. Information Sharing

Professional Radiology PLLC does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in delivering SMS communications, provided they maintain the confidentiality and security of your information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. HIPAA and Healthcare Communications

As a healthcare provider, Professional Radiology PLLC takes patient privacy seriously. SMS messages may contain limited healthcare-related information necessary for appointment coordination and patient care communications. Patients should be aware that text messaging may not always be fully secure.
